Privacy Policy — Talq: Contraction Timer

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Talq does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data.

  • Everything you enter (contractions, kick counts, journal entries, hospital bag, name, due date and provider phone) is saved only on your iPhone and, if you use it, your Apple Watch.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to export a PDF or CSV report and send it yourself.
  • The optional "Talq Supporter" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Deleting the app, or using Settings → Erase all data, removes all of your data.
